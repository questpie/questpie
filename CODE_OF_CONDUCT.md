# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want QUESTPIE to be a project where people are judged on their contributions,
not on who they are. Everyone participating — contributors, maintainers, people
filing issues — is welcome regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings about context.
- Critique code, not people. "This breaks X because Y" is useful; "this is
  garbage" is not.
- Accept that maintainers say no. A rejected PR is not a rejection of you, and
  scope discipline is why the framework stays maintainable.
- Be patient with beginners. Everyone was one.
- Assume everyone is doing this alongside other obligations. Slow replies are not
  disrespect.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks — public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing threads.
- Advocating for or encouraging any of the above.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions — and when someone is representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **dominik@questpie.com**. Reports are
handled confidentially. You will get a response within a week.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions
that violate this document, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose
behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Maintainers who do not enforce this in good faith may face consequences decided by
other maintainers.
